## 5. Scientific and technical validation

- Historical nominal DEG counts reproduced exactly: vascular 440, neuronal
  1,789, neuropil 1,669. Maximum coefficient difference was below 9e-14 and
  maximum p-value difference below 2.1e-12.
- All five primary fits completed, totaling 40,329 screen–gene pairs. All five
  mediator diagnosis gates failed; no pairs qualified as hits.
- All 17 required sensitivity analyses completed with zero hits. SpD07 exclusion
  retained 210 observations/58 donors; outcome universes were 12,509 neuronal
  genes for PTN and 5,097 vascular genes for neuronal FGF1.
- Raw spots exactly reconstructed the original GM pseudobulk count matrices
  and contributing-spot counts. Shared spots comprised 28.00% of matched
  vascular spots in the neuropil comparison and 20.85% in the neuronal comparison.
- Real-data influence, between/within, joint-FGF, and overlap-removal refits were
  not triggered. Synthetic tests covered these branches, sample-loss handling,
  raw-count mismatch rejection, and zero-hit control flow.
- Independent verification recomputed per-screen and pooled primary BH values,
  checked sample/gene identifiers, shared baselines, all sensitivity gene
  universes and counts, and consistency of run signatures and statuses.
- All five PDF pages were rendered and visually inspected. Final renderings
  were pixel-identical to the reviewed preview.

Detailed results and limitations are in `VALIDATION.md` and the archived report.
The full fit objects and caches remain in the JHPCE runtime output directory;
only available report/summary artifacts, provenance configuration, and figures
are included in the review snapshot.
